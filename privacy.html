Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Everything Annotator keeps your highlights and notes on your own computer. It has no accounts, sends nothing to us or anyone else, and doesn't track you.

The extension

Everything Annotator is a browser extension for Firefox, Chrome and Edge.

  • What it stores: your highlights, stickies, the addresses and titles of the pages you annotate, and your settings, such as whether stickies show on the page.
  • Where it stores them: only in your browser's storage for the extension, on your computer.
  • What it sends: nothing. The extension makes no network requests, has no analytics, and contains no advertising.
  • What it reads: the text of the pages you visit, so it can find where your notes belong and show them again. This happens on your computer and is never sent anywhere.
  • Sharing: notes leave your computer only when you choose to. Share annotations and Back up everything save a file, and what happens to that file is up to you.

Browser sync isn't used, so your notes don't follow you to other computers. To remove them, throw them away in All notes, or uninstall the extension, which deletes its storage.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or scripts, and loads no fonts or images from other sites. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and secure the service. GitHub's own privacy statement covers those logs. We don't use them to identify you.

Add-on stores

If you install from Firefox Add-ons, the Chrome Web Store or Edge Add-ons, that store's own privacy policy covers your use of the store.

Children

Everything Annotator collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. A change that collects any data would come with a new version of the extension, described in its release notes.
